## Interaction

Native scroll controls a reversible Hermite camera path and exposure-normalized photographic blends. Scene markers brighten on hover and seek to held compositions when selected. Keyboard selection, skip navigation and replay remain available.

A pointer-led highlight follows the can. Hovering a droplet gently sharpens its lens-like reflection; clicking or tapping the can sends a small focus pulse across the surface. The opening FOCZ wordmark responds with a restrained highlight and slight tracking change, with a matching pulse on click or tap. Reduced motion uses simpler responses. Mobile uses touch pulses and a wider product framing.

## Sound

Off by default. Quiet filtered air follows the playhead; a soft breath accompanies the reflection reveal. Can and wordmark clicks receive a brief tactile tap only after sound is enabled. The score fades into stillness and mutes while the film is offscreen or the page is hidden. Pointer interactions never start audio themselves.

## Rendering

A single procedural sleeve, irregularly distributed clear beads and a few short trails. Droplets use lightweight instancing and surface shading, with no per-frame texture generation or additional transmission render pass. DPR 1–1.5, antialiasing disabled, demand-driven frames and smaller mobile reflections. The still edition uses the supplied FOCZ product photo if the canvas fails.
