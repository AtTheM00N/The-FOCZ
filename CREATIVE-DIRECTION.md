# FOCZ creative direction

## Identity

FOCZ's public identity combines industrial pack typography with outdoor monochrome imagery. The tone is calm, exact and purposeful. The experience is minimal: a cold can, silver light, clear condensation and composed landscapes.

Ink #08090A, bone #E9EAE9, secondary #C3C7C6, muted #8A9092. Thin translucent rules and generous negative space. Geist Sans for restrained display and body text; Geist Mono only for small technical details. A plain FOCZ wordmark with tight tracking.

## Experience architecture

Stillness → cold metal macro → aligned silver marks → gathering dew → horizon reflection → steady can hero → snow / water / terrain → LOCK IN → FOCZ → formula / campaign worlds → launch list.

“A view through the dew” connects scenes 04–10. A slow optical pullback replaces the mechanical chamber. By scene 07 the can is upright and anchored; the landscape changes through soft reflection and photographic blends. Scroll reverses the complete edit without jumps.

The pointer carries a soft highlight across the can and brings a droplet's reflection into focus. Clicking or tapping creates a small surface pulse. The opening wordmark uses a matching restrained highlight, slight spacing change and click pulse. These responses are tactile and brief; they do not move the product around the screen.

## Content

One verified product: Tropical Punch, 355 mL. Ingredient names come from the supplied pack photo. Official campaign images and links form the editorial world. Product details and necessary controls carry the page; repeated “lock in” copy and elaborate campaign slogans are removed. “A prototype by insert_name studios” appears discreetly in the closing scene.

No invented products, formulas, community counts, medical benefits, local availability, commercial contact information or purchase flows. Signup uses the actual official launch destination until a brand-approved backend is connected.

## Polish requirements

The camera reverses without jumps. Type has enough space to stay readable. Clear condensation preserves the label's contrast, and the printed identity remains consistent across environments. Sound is off by default and requires an explicit toggle; enabled interaction taps and the quiet reflection swell stay understated. Keyboard scene selection, skip navigation, native disclosures, reduced motion and WebGL fallback remain usable. Touchscreens use simple tap responses. The lower page works at 320 px and above.
